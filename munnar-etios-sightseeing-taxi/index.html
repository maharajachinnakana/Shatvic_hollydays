Sightseeing list for this direction:

  1. Flower Garden

    A garden stop, subject to opening and entry conditions.

  2. Tea Garden Photo Point

    A roadside tea-estate photo stop near Munnar. Take care around traffic.

  3. Mattupetty Dam and Lake

    Reservoir views about 13 km from town. Boating is separate.

  4. Indo-Swiss Meadows / Shooting Point

    A scenic photo stop on this direction. Local names vary, so confirm the exact map pin before travel.

  5. Echo Point

    A lakeside stop about 15 km from Munnar, known for its echo.

  6. Elephant Arrival Spot

    Wild elephant sightings are never guaranteed. Watch from a safe distance.

  7. Kundala Lake

    A scenic lake about 20 km from Munnar. Boating depends on the operator.

  8. Top Station

    A highland viewpoint about 32 km from Munnar at 1,700 m. Weather decides the view.

Vattavada can be added in a Etios for an extra charge. Jeep safari, tickets and boating are separate.

Direction 2: Anayirangal Dam

A shorter half-day drive of about 22 km along NH 85 toward Chinnakanal, with waterfalls and a boating lake.

Sightseeing list for this direction:

  1. Tea gardens and Lockhart Estate

    The road climbs through tea estates on both sides, with long views on a clear day.

  2. Lockhart Gap viewpoint

    A quick roadside stop for views over the Idukki valleys. Mist can hide the view.

  3. Power House Waterfalls, Chinnakanal

    A roadside waterfall that is strongest during and after the monsoon. Stay behind safety barriers.

  4. Anayirangal Dam and Elephant Arrival Lake

    About 22 km from Munnar on the Kochi-Madurai road (NH 85). Boating is run by Kerala Hydel Tourism and is paid separately. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.

On the way: Cochin to Munnar in a Etios

Coming from Cochin? A Etios suits the 4 to 5 hour drive with up to 4 passengers. Plan short halts at the Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls and the first tea-estate viewpoints. For a full day with 15 stops, see Cochin to Munnar with sightseeing, and for the direct fare see Kochi to Munnar taxi.

Questions people ask

How much is a Etios taxi for Munnar sightseeing?

The Etios fare on the Top Station route is ₹2,300 per vehicle. The Anayirangal Dam, Cochin and Coimbatore directions are ₹2,300 each. Sunrise pickups to Chokramudi, Top Station and Kolukkumalai start at ₹2,000, and Chathurangapara at ₹3,000.

How many people can sit in a Etios?

Up to 4 passengers.

Is there an entry ticket at Top Station?

No. Top Station is an open viewpoint with no ticket.

How much is the Chokramudi entry ticket?

₹500 per head, paid separately from the taxi fare.

How much is the Kolukkumalai jeep?

₹3,200 per jeep for up to 6 persons. The taxi takes you to the jeep point and waits.

How much is the Chathurangapara jeep safari?

₹1,500, paid separately. The sunrise fare covers the taxi leg only.

Is the sunrise guaranteed?

No. Cloud, rain and mist can hide it.

Can I combine two directions in one day?

Sometimes. Anayirangal Dam pairs well with a half day; Top Station needs most of a day.

Is the Etios good for Munnar's roads?

Yes. It is compact, easy on narrow hill roads and simple to park at viewpoints.

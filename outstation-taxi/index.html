How outstation planning works

  1. Send the basics

    Date, pickup, drop, passengers and luggage. Use the Book now button or message us on WhatsApp.

  2. Get a written fare

    We reply with the vehicle, the fare and any extra charge that applies to your plan.

  3. Travel with details in hand

    You know the vehicle, the price and the pickup point before the trip starts.

Fare terms

  • Fares are per private vehicle, not per person.
  • Tolls, parking and driver allowance are included in the confirmed fare.
  • Extra sightseeing, long waiting, trip extensions or vehicle positioning are explained before you confirm.
  • Entry tickets, boating, trekking and jeep safaris are separate unless your confirmed booking includes them.

Questions people ask

How much is a one-way taxi from Kochi to Munnar?

Starting fares per vehicle: Swift Dzire ₹3,500, Etios ₹3,700, Ertiga ₹3,900, Innova ₹4,500 and Innova Crysta or Hycross ₹5,000.

Can I plan a multi-stop route?

Yes. Send every stop and date and we quote the full plan.

Do you offer return trips?

Yes, ask for a return or round trip and we confirm the combined fare.

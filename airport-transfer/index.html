Questions people ask

Which airport should I fly into for Munnar?

Cochin International Airport is the closest. If flights are better into Coimbatore, Madurai or Trivandrum, those routes are also available.

How far is Munnar from Cochin Airport?

About 110 km, usually 3.5 to 4.5 hours depending on traffic and stops.

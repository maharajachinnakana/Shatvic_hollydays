THE DETAILS

Travel with the details in view

Munnar is a hill destination. Weather, road access, opening times and local operators can affect a plan.

✳

Transport is not an activity ticket

Entry tickets, boating, trekking and off-road safaris are separate unless the confirmed booking explicitly includes them.

✳

Starting fares are not final guarantees

Final fare can depend on date, season, vehicle availability and pickup location.

Munnar at a glance

Munnar sits at about 1,600 m in Kerala's Western Ghats. Cochin Airport is about 110 km away, and Kochi to Munnar takes 4 to 5 hours by road. Mornings are clearest for viewpoints such as Top Station, and the monsoon months turn the tea slopes deep green.

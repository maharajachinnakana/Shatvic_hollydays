About the Kovalam to Munnar drive

Kovalam to Munnar links Kerala's southern beaches with its highest tea hills. It is one of the longest private transfers we offer, so plan a full day on the road.

Many guests break the trip at Alleppey or Kochi and finish the next morning. Ask us for a two-leg plan if you prefer that rhythm.

The usual road is via the central Kerala highways.

  1. Alleppey halt

    A scenic midpoint for lunch near the backwaters.

  2. Kochi halt

    Good for a longer stop or an overnight stay.

Which vehicle should you pick?

Swift Dzire and Etios suit up to four passengers; Ertiga fits up to six; the Innova family fits up to seven with more luggage room; Traveller or Urbania suit larger groups. Tell us your passenger count and bags and we will confirm the right vehicle.

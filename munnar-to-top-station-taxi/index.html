GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

How long should I allow?+

Allow most of the day for multiple stops; actual duration depends on traffic, queues, weather and itinerary.

Can we stop at Indo-Swiss Meadows / Shooting Point?+

Request this stop when booking. Since local place names may differ, share a map pin or confirm the exact location with Shadvic Holidays before the trip. The stop depends on road access and the agreed itinerary.

Is Vattavada Jeep Safari included?+

No. It is a separate activity that must be arranged and confirmed separately.

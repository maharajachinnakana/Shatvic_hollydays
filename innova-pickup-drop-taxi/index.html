GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Confirm the details that matter to your journey before you travel.

Is this fare per person or per vehicle?+

Taxi starting fares are quoted per private vehicle unless the confirmed offer states otherwise.

Are tolls, parking and driver allowance included?+

Applicable tolls, parking and driver allowance are included in the confirmed fare. Check the final written quote before booking.

Are sightseeing tickets and activities included?+

Not automatically. Entry tickets, jeep safaris, trekking, boating and other activities are separate unless the confirmed booking explicitly includes them.

Can the final fare change?+

The starting fare may vary with pickup location, date, season, operating conditions and vehicle availability. Any applicable additional charge should be disclosed before confirmation.

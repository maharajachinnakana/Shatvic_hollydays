About the Cochin Airport to Munnar drive

Cochin International Airport at Nedumbassery is the main gateway for Munnar. The road leaves the coastal plain through Perumbavoor and Kothamangalam, then starts to climb beyond Adimali, where tea slopes and hairpin bends take over.

Most guests ask for a direct drop at their resort. If you land late in the day, tell us early: the last stretch is easier in daylight, and mist is common after sunset.

The usual road is via Perumbavoor, Kothamangalam and Adimali on NH 85.

  1. Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls

    Roadside waterfalls on the Kochi to Munnar road past Neriamangalam. They run strongest during and just after the monsoon and make an easy photo halt.

  2. First tea slopes after Adimali

    A short pause here is enough to feel the temperature drop. Munnar town sits at about 1,600 m.

Which vehicle for Cochin Airport to Munnar?

For Cochin Airport to Munnar: Swift Dzire and Etios suit up to four passengers; Ertiga fits up to six; the Innova family fits up to seven with more luggage room. Tell us your passenger count and bags and we will confirm the right vehicle.

Tips for this trip

  • Share your flight number and landing time when you enquire.
  • Keep a light jacket in your hand luggage; evenings in Munnar are cool.
  • Mention heavy luggage or a child seat so the right vehicle is sent.
  • In heavy rain the ghat section is slower, so avoid tight onward plans on the same day.

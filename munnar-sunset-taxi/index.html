Choose a viewpoint

Evenings in Munnar are cool and often misty. Pick the viewpoint that matches how much daylight you have left.

Plan around daylight

  • Leave early enough to reach the viewpoint 45 minutes before sunset.
  • Hill roads are unlit in many sections, so plan the return drive in dusk, not full dark.
  • Kolukkumalai needs a separate local 4WD jeep for the final off-road section.
  • Carry a light jacket; evenings drop quickly.

Questions people ask

Are sunset views guaranteed?

No. Cloud, rain, mist and seasonal conditions can limit visibility.

Is Kolukkumalai Jeep Safari included?

No. It is a separate activity and must be confirmed for the date. The listed local jeep rate is ₹3,200.

Which is the closest sunset point to town?

Idly Hills and Pothamedu View Point are the nearest, both from ₹500 for a Swift Dzire.

## Finding and naming caution

The business-requested name is **Indo-Swiss Meadows Point**, also described by the owner as a **Shooting Point**. This name has been added to the Mattupetty–Kundala–Top Station sightseeing list as requested.

Public web sources consistently identify **Photo Point / Shooting Point** as a tea-garden photography stop on Mattupetty Road near Munnar, while **Indo-Swiss Project / dairy farm** is described as a separate location in the Mattupetty area. The research did not independently verify “Indo-Swiss Meadows Point” as an official map/administrative name. To avoid confusing travellers, the page copy asks customers to confirm the exact map pin and current road access before travel. Do not merge it with the Munnar town-side Tea Garden Photo Point unless the business confirms they are the same stop.

Munnar roads are narrow and winding, so a smaller car is often easier for two or three people. Families with luggage usually prefer an Innova.

Questions people ask

How much is a taxi in Munnar?

It depends on the route and vehicle. A Swift Dzire day on the Mattupetty and Top Station route starts at ₹2,000, and short sunset hops start at ₹500. Airport and outstation fares are on their own pages.

Are Munnar taxi fares per person or per vehicle?

Per private vehicle. A family of four pays the same listed fare as one person in the same car.

Can I book a taxi from my resort?

Yes. Share the exact resort name and drop. If the vehicle must come from elsewhere, any positioning charge is explained before you confirm.

Do you cover places outside Munnar?

Yes. Airports, railway stations and towns such as Thekkady, Alleppey and Kochi have their own pages with starting fares.

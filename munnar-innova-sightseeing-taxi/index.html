Innova sightseeing taxi in Munnar

On the way: Cochin to Munnar in a Innova

Coming from Cochin? A Innova suits the 4 to 5 hour drive with up to 7 passengers. Plan short halts at the Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls and the first tea-estate viewpoints. For a full day with 15 stops, see Cochin to Munnar with sightseeing, and for the direct fare see Kochi to Munnar taxi.

Questions people ask

How much is a Innova taxi for Munnar sightseeing?

The Innova Crysta fare on the Top Station route is ₹3,000 per vehicle, and the Anayirangal Dam, Cochin and Coimbatore directions are ₹3,000 each. Sunrise fares are confirmed on request.

How many people can sit in a Innova?

Up to 7 passengers.

Is there an entry ticket at Top Station on an Innova trip?

No. Top Station is an open viewpoint with no ticket.

How much is the Chokramudi entry ticket on an Innova sunrise trip?

₹500 per head, paid separately from the taxi fare.

How much is the Kolukkumalai jeep after an Innova transfer?

₹3,200 per jeep for up to 6 persons. The taxi takes you to the jeep point and waits.

How much is the Chathurangapara jeep safari after an Innova transfer?

₹1,500, paid separately. The sunrise fare covers the taxi leg only.

Is the sunrise guaranteed on an Innova pickup?

No. Cloud, rain and mist can hide it.

Can an Innova cover two directions in one day?

Sometimes. Anayirangal Dam pairs well with a half day; Top Station needs most of a day.

Is an Innova Crysta better than a Hycross for sightseeing?

They are generations of the same family. Crysta and Hycross offer more comfort; the plain Innova is the most economical. See Innova pickup and drop for every variant.

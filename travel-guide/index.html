Munnar travel guide

Munnar is a hill station at about 1,600 m in Kerala, reached by road from Kochi (about 130 km) in 4 to 5 hours. It is pleasant year-round: March to May is warm, June to September is green and wet, and October to February is cool and misty.

SeasonWhat to expect
March to MayWarm days, clear views, busy season.
June to SeptemberMonsoon, waterfalls and green tea slopes; some roads need care.
October to FebruaryCool, misty mornings; popular for sunrise trips.

Eravikulam National Park closes for several weeks each year during calving season. Check dates before planning.

How to reach Munnar

Munnar has no airport or railway station. The closest airport is Cochin at about 110 km. Most rail travellers arrive at Aluva or Ernakulam and continue by taxi.

What to pack

  • Light jacket or sweater, even in summer.
  • Rain jacket in monsoon.
  • Comfortable shoes for viewpoints.
  • Motion-sickness tablets if you are sensitive to winding roads.

Plan your first day

Start with a sightseeing day and keep a second day for sunrise or a safari enquiry.

Questions people ask

What is the best time to visit Munnar?

Munnar works year-round. Pick March to May for warmth, June to September for greenery, October to February for cool air.

How many days are enough for Munnar?

Two to three days cover the main sights.

Is Munnar safe for families?

Yes. Roads are winding, so plan daylight driving and carry basic medicines.

How do I reach Munnar from Kochi?

By road, about 130 km in 4 to 5 hours. Taxi fares are on the Kochi to Munnar page.

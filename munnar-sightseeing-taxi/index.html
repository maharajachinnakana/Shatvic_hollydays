GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

How long does sightseeing take?+

A full day is common for multiple distant stops; actual duration depends on route, weather, traffic and activities.

Can I include Indo-Swiss Meadows / Shooting Point in Munnar sightseeing?+

Yes, request it for the Mattupetty–Kundala–Top Station direction. Local place names can vary, so confirm the exact map pin and access with Shadvic Holidays before booking.

Are attraction tickets included?+

Not automatically. Tickets and paid activities are separate unless included in the confirmed booking.

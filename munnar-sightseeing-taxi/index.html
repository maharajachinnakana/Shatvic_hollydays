The Mattupetty, Kundala and Top Station route

Stops run in the order you meet them on the road. You can drop any stop or add one; the day is yours.

  1. Flower Garden

    A garden stop, subject to opening and entry conditions.

  2. Tea Garden Photo Point

    A roadside tea-estate photo stop near Munnar. Take care around traffic and stay in permitted areas.

  3. Mattupetty Dam and Lake

    Reservoir views about 13 km from Munnar town. Boating is a separate activity, subject to availability.

  4. Indo-Swiss Meadows / Shooting Point

    A scenic photo stop on this direction. Local place names vary, so confirm the exact map pin and road access before travel.

  5. Echo Point

    A lakeside stop about 15 km from Munnar, known for the echo effect and surrounding hills.

  6. Elephant Arrival Spot

    Wild elephant sightings are not guaranteed. Watch wildlife only from a safe distance.

  7. Kundala Lake

    A scenic lake about 20 km from Munnar. Boating depends on operator availability.

  8. Top Station

    A highland viewpoint about 32 km from Munnar at 1,700 m. Visibility depends on weather; arriving before 9 am gives the best chance of clear views.

Other sightseeing directions

Eravikulam and Rajamala

Home of the Nilgiri tahr, about 13 km from town. The national park closes for several weeks each year during calving season, so check dates before you plan.

Tea Museum and town stops

Tea history, factory visits and shopping near Munnar town. Opening hours vary.

Attukad and Pothamedu

Waterfall and viewpoint stops close to town. Pothamedu is also a popular sunset point.

Chinnakanal and Devikulam

Waterfalls and a quiet lake on side roads from town.

How to plan a good sightseeing day

  • Start by 8 am so you reach Top Station before the mist rises.
  • Pick five or six stops, not twelve. Roads are winding and queues build at popular points.
  • Tell us if anyone in your group has trouble with long drives or steps.
  • Keep tickets, boating and jeep safaris separate in your budget.

What to check before you go

Opening times, entry rules and road access can change with season, weather and local rules. We confirm what is open for your date when you book.

Questions people ask

How long does Munnar sightseeing take?

A full day is common for the Mattupetty to Top Station route. Allow more if you add boating or long photo stops.

Which is the best taxi for Munnar sightseeing?

For two to four people a Swift Dzire or Etios is easy on narrow roads. Families with luggage usually prefer an Innova; groups can ask about a Traveller.

Can I include Indo-Swiss Meadows / Shooting Point?

Yes, request it for the Mattupetty to Top Station direction. Because local place names vary, share a map pin or confirm the exact spot with us before the trip.

Are attraction tickets included in the fare?

No. Entry tickets, boating and safaris are separate unless the confirmed booking says they are included.

What is the best time to visit Munnar for sightseeing?

Munnar works year-round. March to May is warm and clear, June to September is green with waterfalls, and October to February is cool with misty mornings.

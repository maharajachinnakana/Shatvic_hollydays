What to send us

  • Flight or train number and arrival time.
  • Pickup point: terminal, platform exit or hotel name.
  • Passengers, bags and any child seat.
  • Your drop point in Munnar, or the resort name.

Fare terms

  • Fares are per private vehicle, not per person.
  • Tolls, parking and driver allowance are included in the confirmed fare.
  • Extra sightseeing, long waiting, trip extensions or vehicle positioning are explained before you confirm.
  • Entry tickets, boating, trekking and jeep safaris are separate unless your confirmed booking includes them.

Questions people ask

How much is a taxi from the airport to Munnar?

Starting fares per vehicle for a Swift Dzire: Cochin from ₹3,500, Madurai from ₹5,500, Coimbatore from ₹6,000 and Trivandrum from ₹7,500.

Which is the closest airport to Munnar?

Cochin International Airport, about 110 km by road.

Can you pick me up late at night?

Share your landing time and we tell you honestly whether a night drive on hill roads is advisable.

Do you cover other towns?

Yes. See one-way and outstation taxis for Thekkady, Alleppey, Kochi and the south coast.

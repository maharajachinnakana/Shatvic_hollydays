About the Munnar to Thekkady drive

Munnar to Thekkady drops from the tea hills into spice-garden country around Periyar. The road runs down through Poopara and Kattappana, with cardamom estates on both sides, before reaching Vandiperiyar and Thekkady.

Plan for 3 to 4 hours of driving plus any stops. The best weather is usually from September to March, when the air is cool and views are clear.

The usual road is via Poopara, Kattappana and Vandiperiyar.

  1. Cardamom and spice gardens

    Short plantation visits are common on this road. Ask us to include one when you book.

  2. Kattappana and Vandiperiyar

    Useful for meals and fuel on the way down.

Which vehicle for Munnar to Thekkady?

For Munnar to Thekkady: Swift Dzire and Etios suit up to four passengers; Ertiga fits up to six; the Innova family fits up to seven with more luggage room; Traveller or Urbania suit larger groups. Tell us your passenger count and bags and we will confirm the right vehicle.

Tips for this trip

  • Book at least a day ahead in peak season.
  • Mention your Thekkady stay so the drop point is exact.
  • Ask about a Periyar boat or safari plan separately; transport and tickets are different things.
  • Expect winding sections with frequent bends.

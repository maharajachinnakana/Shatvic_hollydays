About the Ernakulam to Munnar drive

Ernakulam Junction and Ernakulam Town are the stations most visitors use when they arrive in Kochi by train. From the city the drive to Munnar runs through Aluva and Perumbavoor before the road starts to rise at Adimali.

City traffic can add time in the first hour, so leave a little earlier on weekdays and festival days.

The usual road is via Aluva, Perumbavoor, Kothamangalam and Adimali on NH 85.

  1. Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls

    Photo halts on the climb. They are at their best after rain.

  2. Adimali

    Last major town before the tea country. Good for a meal and fuel.

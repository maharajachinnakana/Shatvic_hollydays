About this experience

The trek is the activity; our taxi gets you to the start and back. Trails are subject to weather and forest-department rules.

How we help

  • Pre-dawn pickup planning.
  • Return drop to your property.
  • Advice on footwear and water.

What to send us

  • Date and pickup point.
  • Number of people and any children.
  • Vehicle preference and luggage.
  • The exact experience you want, so we can confirm who runs it.

A taxi transfer is not the same as the activity. Jeep safaris, treks, tickets and equipment are separate unless your confirmed booking says they are included. We confirm availability, operator and price before you pay.

Fare terms for the taxi leg

  • Fares are per private vehicle, not per person.
  • Tolls, parking and driver allowance are included in the confirmed fare.
  • Extra sightseeing, long waiting, trip extensions or vehicle positioning are explained before you confirm.
  • Entry tickets, boating, trekking and jeep safaris are separate unless your confirmed booking includes them.

Questions people ask

Is the jeep safari price included in the taxi fare?

No. The taxi transfer and the activity are priced separately unless your confirmed booking says otherwise.

Can a regular taxi do the off-road section?

Not on routes that need a local 4WD jeep for the final stretch. We tell you which parts need one.

Is the experience guaranteed on my date?

No. Road, weather, forest rules and operator availability can change access. We confirm before you pay.

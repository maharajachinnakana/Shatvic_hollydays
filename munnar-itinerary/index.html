Munnar itinerary for 1, 2 and 3 days

A Munnar trip works best as one direction a day: Top Station on day one, Anayirangal Dam on day two, and the Cochin or Coimbatore road on day three. A taxi day starts at ₹2,000 for a Swift Dzire, ₹2,300 for an Etios and ₹3,000 for an Innova Crysta.

Day by day

These days use the four sightseeing directions. Details and stop lists are on Munnar sightseeing.

  1. Day 1: Top Station direction

    Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, Kundala Lake and Top Station. Start by 8 am so the mist has not closed the viewpoint.

  2. Day 2: Anayirangal Dam direction

    A shorter drive of about 22 km along NH 85 with Lockhart Gap, Power House Waterfalls and the dam. Pair it with a slow tea-garden afternoon.

  3. Day 3: Cochin or Coimbatore road

    Waterfalls and tea views toward Cochin, or Marayoor and the Chinnar forest road toward Tamil Nadu.

If you arrive from Cochin, turn the road into day zero with the Cochin to Munnar sightseeing plan, from ₹6,000 per vehicle. Reaching Munnar by afternoon still leaves the evening free.

Questions people ask

Is one day enough for Munnar?

One day covers one direction, usually Top Station. Two days cover most first-visit sights.

What is the best 2-day Munnar plan?

Day one Top Station direction, day two Anayirangal Dam direction. Add a sunrise start on either day if you like early mornings.

Can I do two directions in one day?

Anayirangal Dam is a half day, so it can sometimes pair with a short town stop. Top Station needs most of a day.

How do I fit Kolukkumalai into a plan?

Kolukkumalai is a pre-dawn trip with a jeep. Keep it as a separate morning and rest in the afternoon.

About the Kochi to Munnar drive

Kochi to Munnar is Kerala's classic road trip: coastal city to cool hill station in a single morning. The road is well maintained, with clear signboards, and most of the climb happens in the last 40 km.

A private taxi lets you leave when you like, stop where you like, and arrive at your property's door. Ghat sections need extra care during the monsoon, from June to September.

The usual road is via Aluva, Perumbavoor, Kothamangalam and Adimali on NH 85.

  1. Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls

    Roadside waterfalls that suit a short photo halt.

  2. Tea estates near Munnar

    The slopes beyond Adimali are among the first tea views on the route.

Which vehicle for Kochi to Munnar?

For Kochi to Munnar: Swift Dzire and Etios suit up to four passengers; Ertiga fits up to six; the Innova family fits up to seven with more luggage room. Tell us your passenger count and bags and we will confirm the right vehicle.

Tips for this trip

  • Leave Kochi before 9 am to enjoy the hill road in daylight.
  • Share your hotel name for the pickup fare.
  • Pack a jacket; the hill air is much cooler.
  • Ask about a combined Kochi to Munnar to Thekkady plan.

How far is Munnar from Kochi?

About 130 km by road. It usually takes 4 to 5 hours depending on traffic, weather and stops.

Is it better to take a taxi or the bus?

A private taxi gives door-to-door timing and room for luggage. Buses are cheaper but slower and fixed to station stops.

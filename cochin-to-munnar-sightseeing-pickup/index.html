How the trip works

  • We pick up your group at the Cochin airport, a hotel or a station.
  • On the way we stop for food, tea and photos. You set the pace.
  • Need a rest in between? Tell the driver and we stop. The drive is flexible for children and older guests.
  • We drop you at your stay in Munnar.

This is a private taxi with sightseeing, not a fixed tour. You choose the stops and how long you spend at each.

15 stops on the way and around Munnar

Pick the stops you like. You do not need to see them all. Starting with the two waterfalls, the list goes on toward Munnar.

  1. Cheeyappara Waterfall

    A tiered waterfall beside the highway, about 38 km before Munnar. Best from September to March. No entry fee; the rocks are slippery.

  2. Valara Waterfall

    About 1 km from Cheeyappara and usually visited with it. Strongest after rain.

  3. Zipline (1.8 km)

    An adventure activity with a paid ticket. Confirm timings and limits with the operator.

  4. Sky swing

    A paid adventure ride. Check age and weight limits at the counter.

  5. Spice garden

    A short plantation visit for pepper, cardamom, clove and cinnamon.

  6. Elephant safari

    A paid activity subject to availability. Never approach wild elephants.

  7. Glass bridge

    A paid viewpoint attraction over the valley. Check opening hours and weather.

  8. Sengulam Dam

    A quiet reservoir viewpoint. Access and opening times vary.

  9. Chocolate factory

    A stop for fresh homemade chocolate, a favourite with children.

  10. Dream Land Adventure Park

    A paid adventure park. Confirm rides and timings before you go.

  11. Kerala saree handloom factory

    A shop and weaving stop for handloom sarees and gifts.

  12. Hot air balloon

    A paid experience, subject to weather and operator availability.

  13. 2nd Mile viewpoint

    A tea-garden viewpoint close to Munnar town.

  14. Attukad Waterfall

    A scenic fall near Munnar, strongest after the monsoon. Stay behind safety barriers.

  15. Hydel Park

    A garden and viewpoint near Munnar.

Zipline, sky swing, elephant safari, glass bridge, hot air balloon and the adventure park are paid activities. Tickets are separate from the taxi fare, and operating hours change with season and weather. For meals and tea, Kothamangalam, Neriamangalam and Adimali all have stops.

After you reach Munnar: sightseeing directions

Once you have reached Munnar, only the sightseeing points change. Pick any of the three directions below, or ask us to combine them over two days.

Direction 1: Top Station

Mattupetty, Echo Point, Kundala Lake and Top Station, the classic Munnar day.

  • Mattupetty Dam, about 13 km from town.
  • Echo Point and Kundala Lake on the same road.
  • Top Station, about 32 km from Munnar at 1,700 m. Arrive before 9 am for the clearest views.

Full stop list and fares: Munnar to Top Station taxi.

Direction 2: Anayirangal Dam

A half-day drive of about 22 km toward Chinnakanal.

  • Tea gardens and the Lockhart Gap viewpoint.
  • Power House Waterfalls near Chinnakanal.
  • Anayirangal Dam and Elephant Arrival Lake. Boating is paid separately.

See the sightseeing options on Etios sightseeing in Munnar.

Direction 3: Marayoor and the Coimbatore road

A scenic drive east toward Tamil Nadu.

  • Marayoor, known for sandalwood forest and ancient dolmens.
  • The Chinnar forest road. Follow local halting rules.
  • A meal stop near Udumalpet before the plains.

Heading on to Coimbatore? See Munnar to Coimbatore sightseeing.

Is Cochin to Munnar sightseeing taxi worth it?

Yes, if you do not want to rush. A direct drive takes 4 to 5 hours, and a few unhurried halts add 1 to 2 hours. You see the waterfalls in daylight and arrive rested.

What are the best places to visit between Kochi and Munnar?

Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls are the two most people stop for. Then come the spice gardens and adventure stops, the 2nd Mile viewpoint, Attukad Waterfall and Hydel Park near Munnar.

How long does Kochi to Munnar take with stops?

Plan 5 to 7 hours door to door with a meal and two or three halts. Leaving Cochin before 9 am keeps the hill section in daylight.

Can we stop for food and tea on the way?

Yes. Tell the driver when you want a break. There are tea and meal stops in Kothamangalam, Neriamangalam and Adimali.

Can the taxi pick up from Cochin Airport?

Yes. Share your flight number and landing time and we plan a pickup with sightseeing. See Cochin Airport to Munnar taxi for the direct fare.

What does the price depend on?

The vehicle, the date, the number of stops and the waiting time. Fares start at ₹6,000 per vehicle. Compare the direct fare on Kochi to Munnar taxi.

Questions people ask

How much is Cochin to Munnar taxi with sightseeing?

Starting from ₹6,000 per vehicle. The price changes with the vehicle and the stops you choose.

Which waterfalls are on the way from Kochi to Munnar?

Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls, which are about 1 km apart on the highway near Adimali.

Is there an entry fee at Cheeyappara?

No. The main falls are visible from the road. Take care near the slippery rocks and the busy highway curve.

What is the best time to visit Cheeyappara and Valara?

September to March, after the monsoon, when the water is strong and the roads are safer.

Can I take rest stops if someone feels unwell?

Yes. The drive is flexible, and we stop whenever the group needs a break.

Is the drop at my resort?

Yes. We drop you at your stay in Munnar.

Is the fare per person?

No. Fares are per private vehicle.

Are tolls and parking included?

Tolls, parking and driver allowance are included in the confirmed fare. Entry tickets and boating are separate.

Can I do sightseeing in Munnar after the drive?

Yes. Choose Top Station, Anayirangal Dam or the Marayoor road, and we confirm timing and fare.

Is it safe to drive to Munnar at night?

We advise against late-night hill driving. Plan to reach Munnar in daylight.

Munnar to Cochin Airport and Cochin

Cochin International Airport is about 110 km from Munnar, a drive of roughly 3.5 to 4.5 hours. Leave at least 6 hours before an international flight and 5 hours before a domestic one, to allow for traffic and halts. Share your flight time and we plan the pickup time with a safe buffer.

  • Pickup from your resort or hotel in Munnar.
  • Plan for waterfall and tea halts only if your flight time allows.
  • Details: Cochin Airport and Munnar taxi.

Munnar to Aluva and Ernakulam railway stations

Munnar has no railway station. Most guests take a drop to Aluva or Ernakulam Junction to catch trains. Tell us your train number and departure time, and we plan the pickup so you reach the station comfortably.

Compare fares: Aluva and Ernakulam.

Munnar to Cherai

Cherai is a beach on Vypin island near Kochi. A Munnar drop to Cherai is longer than a drop to Cochin city, so ask us for the exact fare and arrival time. We quote it on request.

Munnar to Alleppey

Alleppey is about 168 km from Munnar and takes around 4 hours 45 minutes by road. It is the usual drop for backwater houseboats. See Alleppey and Munnar taxi for starting fares.

Munnar to Kumarakom

Kumarakom is a backwater village known for houseboats and bird watching. We quote Munnar to Kumarakom drops on request. If you also want Alleppey, ask for a combined plan.

Munnar to Varkala

Varkala is a cliff beach town near Thiruvananthapuram and a long drive from Munnar. Start early so the hill section is in daylight. See Munnar to Varkala taxi for starting fares.

Munnar to Bodi (Bodinayakanur)

Bodinayakanur, also called Bodi, is a Tamil Nadu town near the Kerala border, used by guests heading on to Madurai or Theni. See Bodinayakanur and Munnar taxi for starting fares.

Munnar to Thekkady

Thekkady is about 90 km from Munnar, usually 3 to 4 hours. It is known for the Periyar area, spice gardens and boating. See Munnar to Thekkady taxi for starting fares.

What to send us for a drop

  • Drop point and the date.
  • Number of passengers and luggage.
  • Flight or train number and time, if any.
  • Your pickup stay in Munnar.

Which vehicle for a Munnar drop?

Swift Dzire and Etios suit up to four passengers; Ertiga fits up to six; Innova family fits up to seven with more luggage space. Tell us your group and bags and we confirm the right one.

Questions people ask

How much is a Munnar to Cochin Airport taxi?

From the starting fare listed in the table on this page, per vehicle. Choose your vehicle and we confirm in writing.

How long is the drive from Munnar to Cochin Airport?

About 110 km, usually 3.5 to 4.5 hours depending on traffic and halts.

Do you drop at Aluva and Ernakulam railway stations?

Yes. Share your train number and time and we plan the pickup.

Can you drop me at Alleppey for a houseboat?

Yes. Alleppey is about 168 km from Munnar, around 4 hours 45 minutes.

Do you drop at Cherai and Kumarakom?

Yes. Fares for these are quoted on request.

How long does Munnar to Thekkady take?

About 90 km, usually 3 to 4 hours.

Is the Munnar drop fare per person?

No. Fares are per private vehicle.

Can we stop on the way for sightseeing?

Yes. See our sightseeing pickup page for the waterfalls and stops on the Cochin road.

Is it safe to start a drop late at night?

We advise against late-night hill driving. Plan to leave Munnar in daylight when you can.

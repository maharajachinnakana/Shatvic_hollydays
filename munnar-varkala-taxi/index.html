About the Munnar to Varkala drive

Munnar to Varkala is a hills-to-cliffs transfer, from tea country to a beach town known for its red cliffs. It is a long drive, so we suggest a full day or a break at Alleppey or Kochi.

Varkala's cliff area is busy in the evening, so tell us where you are staying and we plan the final approach.

The usual road is via the central Kerala highways.

  1. Alleppey or Kochi break

    A good midpoint for a meal or an overnight stay.

  2. Varkala cliff

    Evenings are best, with sea views and cafes along the cliff path.

Which vehicle for Munnar to Varkala?

For Munnar to Varkala: Swift Dzire and Etios suit up to four passengers; Ertiga fits up to six; the Innova family fits up to seven with more luggage room; Traveller or Urbania suit larger groups. Tell us your passenger count and bags and we will confirm the right vehicle.

Tips for this trip

  • Start early in the morning.
  • Share your Varkala hotel name.
  • Plan at least one proper meal break.
  • Consider splitting the trip over two days.
